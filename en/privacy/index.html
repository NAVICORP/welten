This Privacy Policy is governed primarily by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection. The European General Data Protection Regulation will also be observed where it applies to a particular processing activity.

2. Visiting the website

This website is hosted and delivered through Vercel. When you visit the website, Vercel and the technical infrastructure used to deliver the site may process certain information, including:

  • IP address
  • date and time of access
  • page requested
  • browser, operating system and device type
  • source of the request
  • technical error and security information

This information is required to provide the website securely and reliably.

Welten Communication does not currently use a separate website analytics service, advertising pixels or cross-site tracking. Technical server and security logs may nevertheless be created by the hosting and infrastructure providers for the secure and reliable operation of the website.

3. Contacting us

If you contact us through the contact form or by email, we process the information you provide. This may include your name, email address, company and the contents of your message.

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare a possible consulting relationship and conduct the related communication.

The contact form is technically processed through Web3Forms and the submitted information is forwarded to anna@weltencommunication.ch. Subsequent email communication is handled through Google Workspace.

Your information will not automatically be used for newsletters or other direct marketing.

4. Booking a call

This website integrates links and technical components for appointment scheduling through Google Calendar. The booking page is opened when you choose to book a conversation.

When you open the booking page or make an appointment, Google may process technical connection data and the information you submit in accordance with its own privacy policy. Welten Communication receives the information required to arrange the appointment and uses it solely to organise and conduct the call.

5. Service providers and international processing

We use the following principal service providers to operate the website and process enquiries:

  • Vercel for website hosting and delivery
  • Web3Forms for processing the website contact form
  • Google for Google Workspace email, Google Calendar appointment scheduling and Google Fonts
  • Cloudflare for DNS infrastructure

These providers may process information in Switzerland, the European Union, the United States or other countries. Where a country does not provide an adequate level of data protection, transfers are made subject to appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

Personal data is not sold and is only disclosed where necessary for the purposes described above or to comply with a legal obligation.

6. Cookies and tracking

The website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, tracking pixels or retargeting technologies. A language preference may be stored locally in your browser solely to remember your selected website language. Google Calendar and other external services may use cookies or similar technologies when their functions are opened or used.

Where consent is legally required for a non-essential technology, it will be requested before that technology is activated. This Privacy Policy will be updated if additional analytics, marketing or embedded third-party services are introduced.

7. Retention and security

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, manage a possible engagement or comply with legal obligations. It is subsequently deleted or anonymised.

We and our service providers use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. The website is delivered using encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable data-protection law, you may request information about your personal data and ask for inaccurate information to be corrected or deleted. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent with future effect.

To exercise your rights or ask a privacy-related question, contact: anna@weltencommunication.ch.

This Privacy Policy may be amended if the website, the services used or the applicable legal requirements change.
